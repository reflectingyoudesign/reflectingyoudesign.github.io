Privacy Policy

Last updated Sep 22, 2026 · Applies to our Pinterest API integration
In short

This is an internal tool used only by Reflecting You Design to publish Pins on our own Pinterest Business account. We don't collect data from anyone else, we don't run ads, and we don't share tokens or content with any third party.

01Who we are

Reflecting You Design ("we", "us") is a print-on-demand shop selling art prints and gifts on Etsy. We operate a private internal tool that connects to the Pinterest API to create and publish Pins promoting our own products — it is not a public-facing app or service used by other people.

02What we collect

  • OAuth tokens. When we authorize our own Pinterest Business account, Pinterest issues an access token and a refresh token, which our tool stores to make authenticated API requests.
  • Board information. We read the list of boards on our own connected account, so we can choose where a Pin gets published.

We do not collect data about Pinterest users, visitors, or any third party. There are no end users of this tool besides our own team.

03How we use it

Tokens and board data are used exclusively to authenticate requests to the Pinterest API and to publish Pins. Pin content — images, titles, descriptions, and hashtags — is generated from photographs of our own products and is never derived from, or applied to, anyone else's account or data.

04Storage & security

Tokens are stored in our application's local database, accessible only to our own team. We do not transmit or sell this data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or analytics of any kind.

05Third-party sharing

We don't share Pinterest data, tokens, or generated content with any third party, advertiser, or data broker. The only party this data is exchanged with is Pinterest's own API, to publish content on our behalf.

06Cookies & tracking

This policy page and the underlying tool set no cookies and run no analytics or tracking scripts.

07Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "last updated" date above. Since this is an internal tool with no external users, changes are typically minor clarifications rather than changes in practice.
